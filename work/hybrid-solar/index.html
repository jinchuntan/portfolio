The problem

Sustainable lighting calls for solutions that connect energy engineering with real-world implementation.

My contribution

I led a five-member team in developing the hybrid solar lighting concept at Monash University Malaysia, using TRIZ and the Kanawa-Tsugi concept to guide the design.

The outcome

The project was nominated for the James Dyson Award 2021 and featured by Springwise. It was selected for Youth Sustainable Energy Hub Cohort 4 in August 2026. The public initiative listing describes testing and prototyping.

Scope & status

Recognition describes the project and selection record; it does not imply commercial deployment.
